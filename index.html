Manufacturing Parameters & Print Optimisation

Parameter
Range / Setting
Engineering Observation
Layer Resolution
Extra Fine → Fast
Fine profiles improved dimensional consistency and edge quality.
Infill Density
10% → 40%
Higher infill improved structural rigidity during muscle press testing.
Print Speed
40 → 50 mm/s
Higher speeds reduced quality around QR geometry and snap-fit edges.
Support Structures
Enabled / Disabled
Support removal occasionally damaged surface finish and tolerances.
Ironing
Mostly Disabled
Produced unwanted surface layers affecting fit quality.
Brim Width
10 → 30 mm
Large brim improved bed adhesion and reduced warping risk.
Material
PLA
Required by project specification and used across all final iterations.
